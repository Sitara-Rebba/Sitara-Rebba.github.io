Lucy Gray Baird is apart of the Covey family. Both her parents died, and she was raised by a rando old man. The covey has a special way of naming their own! Their 1st name come from a traditional poem/song/ballad, and their 2nd name comes from a color, but Lucy Gray was special! her name come from the ballad: Lucy Gray by William Wordworth, so it already has a color in it too! 2. Haymitch Abernathy

Haymitch Abernathy is the winner of the 50th Hunger Games from District 12. He was chosen as a tribute for the Second Quarter Quell, where there were twice as many tributes. He was clever, sarcastic, and incredibly good at figuring out how things worked. During the Games, Haymitch discovered that the arena's force field could be used against his opponents. He used it to defeat the final tribute and become the victor. But Haymitch's victory came at a terrible cost. The Capitol punished him by killing the people he cared about, and he was left to live with the consequences of winning. His mother and young brother, Sid, died in a fire ordered by President Snow on the night of Haymitch's arrival. His girlfriend did throught bloodred poisonous gumdrops sent by President Snow Years later, he became the mentor for District 12 and helped Katniss and Peeta survive the Games. Honestly, Haymitch is one of my favourites because he isn't just a rascal, drunk idiot, and a sarcastic mentor. He is someone who survived something horrible and learned to hide a lot of his pain behind his attitude.

His Games: The 50th Hunger Games — The Second Quarter Quell
